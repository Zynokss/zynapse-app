# Getting started

These steps describe the Android 1.0.0 build 9 preview. Public installation is pending review.

1. **Set up your preferences.** Enter a display name, choose English, French or Arabic, select your currency and appearance, and choose whether to enable reminders. Reminders depend on device support and permissions.
2. **Choose a habit.** Pick a preset or a custom habit. Enter a typical daily baseline and your goals. Cost-related savings use the baseline you enter and are estimates.
3. **Take the app guide.** The first-time guide introduces the main screens. You can skip it and replay it from **Profile → Settings → App guide**.
4. **Use Home.** Review the selected habit's streak and available daily actions. Change the selected habit when you track more than one.
5. **Record what happened.** The **+** button includes mood, craving, journal and slip actions, as well as adding a habit. A slip can be recorded without resetting the streak; choose the reset option only if that is what you intend.
6. **Get support in the moment.** A craving log opens coping support. Tools also provides breathing, grounding, urge surfing and a timer. Follow the prompts and record how you feel afterward.
7. **Reflect and review.** Write a Journal note or save a reason for change. Progress summarizes recorded events; missing entries are not automatically treated as failures.
8. **Meet your companion.** The fox grows with your journey. Open its page for Feed and Play, or browse available cosmetics in the shop. Feed and Play are temporary interactions rather than purchases or recovery scores.

The top section controls provide access to Home, Progress, Journal, Tools and Journey. The lower dock provides Home, Progress, Journal and **+** shortcuts. Profile leads to Settings, where you can change language and appearance, replay the guide and export selected data.

Local tracking does not require an account. Do not rely on optional cloud features as your only backup in this preview. Read [backup and restore](BACKUP_AND_RESTORE.md) before changing devices.
